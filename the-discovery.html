Inspired by Concussion by Jeanne Marie Laskas

ABOUT

You are Dr. Ben Okoye, a forensic pathologist newly arrived in America. Working in a county coroner's office, you uncover a pattern of brain damage in retired professional football players — and face an institution unwilling to hear it.

Phase 1 — The Field: Arrow keys / WASD / touch to run. Take hits or truck through them.
Phase 2 — The Lab: Drag to pan, click/tap to find evidence. Toggle the stain.
Phase 3 — The Pushback: Choose your words. Every answer has a cost.

This is a work of fiction using invented names and a fictional league. It honors real people who suffered from CTE.
